# Privacy Policy — Google Meet Transcript

**Effective date:** 11 September 2026

## In short

Everything this extension records stays on your own computer. It has no server, sends no
network requests of any kind, and nobody — including the developer — can see your transcripts.

## What the extension handles

While you have a recording running, it reads the caption text Google Meet already displays on
the meeting page and saves it. That means:

- **What was said**, as Meet's captions transcribe it.
- **Speaker names**, exactly as Meet labels them on screen.
- **Meeting code and title**, taken from the meeting page.
- **Timestamps** of when each line was spoken.

It reads nothing else from the page, and it runs on `meet.google.com` only. It does not access
your camera, your microphone, your audio, your Google account, your email, your other tabs or
any other website. It never starts on its own: a recording begins only when you press
**Start recording**, and ends when you press stop or close the meeting tab.

## Where it is stored

In your browser's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`), on the device you are
using, inside your Chrome profile. Transcripts are not synced to your Google account and do
not follow you to other devices.

## What is transmitted

Nothing. The extension makes no network requests. There is no server, no cloud storage, no
account, no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no advertising and no third-party
service of any kind. Its only permissions are local storage and access to `meet.google.com`
pages, and it requests no ability to send data anywhere.

Your transcripts leave your device only if you choose to export one with **Copy** or
**Download** and then send it somewhere yourself.

## How long it is kept, and how to delete it

Transcripts are kept until you delete them. You can delete any transcript with the **Delete**
button in the extension's popup. Removing the extension from Chrome deletes everything it
stored.

## Sharing and selling

Your data is never sold, shared, transferred or disclosed to anyone, because it never leaves
your device in the first place.

## Recording other people

The extension transcribes what everyone in the meeting says, not only you. Whether you may do
that, and whether you must tell the other participants first, depends on where you and they
are. In the EU, transcribing identifiable people's speech is processing their personal data
under the GDPR, and you — not the developer — are the controller of anything you record.
Telling the meeting before you start is the safe default, and in many situations it is
required.

## Children

The extension is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone, of any age,
beyond what is described above.
